2. Authorize with Twitch

One-time setup
Token received and saved!

Your Twitch token is stored in this browser. The game can now reply in chat as you.

To let the game reply in your chat (e.g. “@viewer drove RED out in 12 moves”), you need to authorize it with your Twitch account. It’s a one-time 4-step process — no server or coding needed.

  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps/create and log in as @1hasank1.
  2. Fill in the registration form:
    • Name: anything (e.g. Tiny Gridlock)
    • OAuth Redirect URL: copy-paste this exactly (all three lines — this page, and the game under both of its addresses). Twitch checks the address it returns to word for word, and the game opens as …/, …/index.html or …/twitch.html depending on where you start.
    • Category: Game Integration
  3. Click Create, then copy the Client ID that appears and paste it in the field below.
  4. Click Authorize with Twitch below — log in and approve — you’ll be sent back here with your token saved automatically!
